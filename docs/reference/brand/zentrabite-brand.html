Who we are

A website and CRM for local businesses. Their own site takes the orders. Their customer list brings people back.

No commission

No cut of direct orders. Businesses pay a monthly fee and Stripe's card fees.

Your customers are yours

Every customer and their history belongs to the business, ready to export any time.

Built in Adelaide

For restaurants, cafés, shops, salons, gyms and trades. Food is the first and clearest example, not the only one.

How we write

Do

  • Short sentences in plain Australian English.
  • Say the specific thing the product does.
  • One main action, worded exactly "Book a call".
  • One secondary action, "Try the live demo".
  • Caption product pictures "Our demo restaurant, sample data."
  • Send price questions to the pricing page.

Don't

  • Em dashes.
  • Buzzwords like unlock, seamless, empower, all-in-one, on autopilot.
  • Rhythmic slogans like "Own your customers. Own your margins."
  • Invented stats, testimonials, logos or results.
  • Demo data shown as if it were a real customer.
  • Anything that says sign-ups are open. They open at launch, with a 1-month free trial.

Colour

Zentra Green is the main colour. The other greens are shades of it. Navy and white do most of the work around it.

Zentra Green#00AD82
Buttons, the green band, links on dark, active states.

Why it changed on 12 Nov

The old green, #00B67A, was too close to Uber Eats green, and it was exactly Trustpilot's five-star green. On a standard colour difference score it was 6 from Uber Eats. #00AD82 is 10, which reads as a different green at a glance, but it's only a small step from our old one.

The look

Website

  • Light only. White page, navy only for the closing section and the footer.
  • Surfaces are told apart by a step in tone, not by lines.
  • Real product screens from the demo restaurant, never mock-ups or device frames.
  • Only pictures move, quick and short (about 300 to 450ms). Words never fade in. Nothing moves for people who ask for less motion.
  • On phones, text inputs are 16px or more, tap targets 44px or more, and nothing scrolls sideways at 320px.

Never on the website

  • Small labels above headings, numbered steps or 01/02 counters.
  • Thin dividing lines and 1px outlined boxes.
  • Gradients, glows, frosted glass, or coloured words in headlines.
  • Shadows on things at rest. Only things that float get one.
  • Rows of cards with an icon on top, and emoji.

App (CRM)

  • A dark shell. #0F1F2D background, #1C2D48 cards, green for what's active and good news.
  • The demos on the website show the app exactly as it looks, so they stay dark too.

Icons
